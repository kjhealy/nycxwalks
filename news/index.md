# Changelog

## nycxwalks 0.0.1.9000

- Initial release.

- New datasets `nyc_xwalk_df` (long-format crosswalk with intersection
  areas and shares of both features), `nyc_xwalk_wide_df` (wide-format
  crosswalk), `nyc_xwalk_geographies_df` (sources and releases), and
  `nyc_xwalk_boundaries_sf` (the boundaries used), for fifteen NYC
  geographies. Built with nycbuildxwalks 0.0.0.9001 on 2026-10-24.

- [`nyc_xwalk()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycxwalks/reference/nyc_xwalk.md)
  returns the crosswalk between two geographies.
